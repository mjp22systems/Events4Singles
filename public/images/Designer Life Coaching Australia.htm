What is Life Coaching?

Coaching is about realising your potential to maximise performance and life satisfaction.  It is a joint working relationship that supports you to understand and learn new techniques to enhance or redirect your life, career and organisational skills.  

Coaching is focused and defined conversations that unleash clarity, confidence and direction. Coaching is not therapy, counseling or psychology.   The coaching process helps the client ‘discover their own advice’.

This is why coaching is so effective.

What is involved?       

Depending on the clients needs, we tailor a program to suit the individual’s requirements.  Our most popular life coaching programs consist of 6 x 1 hour consultations conducted face to face, containing various life and skill audits, and unlimited phone/email support for the duration of the program (up to 6 months or longer if required) which maximises follow up and goal achievement. This program is solution based and outcome focused which compliments the motivation of the client.
